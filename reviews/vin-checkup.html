Product Overview

VIN Checkup is an online vehicle history report service that allows anyone to look up the full background of any vehicle using nothing more than its 17-character Vehicle Identification Number. The service aggregates data from government databases, insurance records, salvage yards, auto auctions, and law enforcement agencies to compile a comprehensive report that reveals a vehicle's true past.

It currently holds the number one spot in the Software and Services category on ClickBank's top offers. The core value proposition is straightforward: before you hand over your hard-earned money for a used car, spend a few dollars and a few minutes to find out whether that car has a checkered past.

What sets VIN Checkup apart is its availability on the ClickBank marketplace, which means it comes with a solid money-back guarantee and is backed by ClickBank's customer-friendly refund policies. It positions itself as an affordable, fast, and accessible alternative to pricier competitors like Carfax.

What's Included

Title and Ownership History

Number of previous owners, duration of each ownership period, and states where the vehicle was registered. Crucial for spotting title washing — the illegal practice of re-registering a salvage vehicle in a different state.

Salvage and Total Loss Records

Flags vehicles that have been declared a total loss by insurance companies, pulling from insurance industry databases and salvage auction records.

Accident and Damage Reports

Compiles accident data from police reports, insurance claims, and collision repair facilities. Major incidents that affect safety and value are typically captured.

Odometer Readings and Rollback Detection

Tracks recorded mileage over time and flags discrepancies that may indicate tampering. Over 450,000 vehicles are sold each year with rolled-back odometers.

Flood and Fire Damage

Cross-references FEMA records, insurance claims, and salvage data to identify water or fire damage history.

Lien and Repossession Records

Outstanding loans or liens that could result in repossession even after purchase.

Recall Information

Open safety recalls from the manufacturer and NHTSA.

Vehicle Use and Specifications

Factory specs (make, model, year, engine, trim) plus prior use as taxi, rental car, police vehicle, or fleet vehicle.

✓ Pros

  • Affordable pricing — Significantly lower than Carfax's $40+ per report, making it practical to run checks on several vehicles without breaking the bank.
  • Instant results — Complete vehicle history report generated within seconds. No waiting for emails or calling customer service.
  • Comprehensive data from multiple sources — Aggregates from state DMVs, insurance carriers, salvage auctions, law enforcement, and federal databases for the same core data as pricier competitors.
  • ClickBank 60-day money-back guarantee — If unsatisfied for any reason, request a full refund within 60 days. Essentially removes the risk from trying the service.
  • User-friendly interface — Clean, straightforward design with no confusing subscription tiers or jargon-heavy output. Even first-time buyers can understand the report.

✗ Cons

  • Not every incident will appear — Minor repairs at independent shops paid in cash may never show up. A clean report is a positive signal, but not an absolute guarantee. Always get an independent mechanical inspection.
  • Brand recognition not as strong as Carfax — Carfax has spent decades building brand awareness. Presenting a VIN Checkup report in a negotiation may carry less weight with some sellers.
  • Limited subscription or bulk-pricing options — Primarily geared toward individual consumers doing one-off checks. High-volume users may find better value elsewhere.
  • No dealership integration — You won't see VIN Checkup included as a standard offering at used car lots like Carfax is.

Who Is This For?

  • First-time used car buyers — An easy, affordable way to level the playing field and avoid making a thousand-dollar mistake on your first vehicle purchase.
  • Private-party shoppers — If you're buying on Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace, or local classifieds, a VIN Checkup report gives you objective data the seller may not volunteer.
  • Used car dealers — Small and independent dealers can use VIN Checkup as a cost-effective preliminary screening tool for evaluating trade-ins and auction purchases.
  • People selling a used car — Having a clean vehicle history report to show buyers demonstrates transparency, builds trust, and can justify a higher asking price.

The Bottom Line

VIN Checkup is a practical, affordable, and reliable tool that does exactly what it promises: it gives you a clear picture of a used vehicle's history so you can make a more informed buying decision. The price is accessible, the reports are generated instantly, and the data coverage for the most important categories — title issues, salvage records, accident history, odometer discrepancies, flood damage, and liens — is solid.

For the vast majority of used car buyers, VIN Checkup hits the sweet spot of value and functionality. In a market where the cost of not checking a vehicle's history can be measured in thousands of dollars, the small investment in a VIN Checkup report is one of the smartest moves you can make.
