Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

1. Who We Are

Sweet Glider operates the website sweetglider.online and the Sweet Glider Pinterest content publishing application. We are a content publishing service that creates and distributes honest product reviews and recommendations through the Pinterest platform using the official Pinterest Content API.

2. Information We Collect

Sweet Glider collects minimal personal information necessary to operate our service. Specifically, we collect Pinterest account authentication credentials (OAuth tokens) for the sole purpose of publishing content to Pinterest on behalf of authorized users. We do not collect browsing history, personal demographics, or financial information beyond what is required for API authentication.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to publish content to Pinterest on behalf of authorized users via the Pinterest Content API. This includes creating boards, posting pins, and managing content. We do not sell, share, rent, or distribute your personal information to any third parties for marketing or any other purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

All authentication tokens and credentials are stored securely using industry-standard encryption methods. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel who need it to operate the service.

5. Third-Party Services

Our service integrates with the Pinterest Content API v5. Pinterest's privacy policy can be found at https://policy.pinterest.com/en/privacy-policy. We are not responsible for Pinterest's data collection or privacy practices. We encourage you to review Pinterest's privacy policy when using our service.

6. Affiliate Marketing Disclosure

Sweet Glider participates in affiliate marketing programs, including the ClickBank affiliate program. This means we may earn commissions on products purchased through links in our content, at no additional cost to you. All affiliate relationships are clearly disclosed in our content. Our reviews represent our honest opinions and are not influenced by affiliate commissions.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. You may revoke Pinterest API access at any time through your Pinterest account settings. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at sweetglider1@gmail.com.

8. Data Retention

We retain your authentication data only for as long as necessary to provide our service. If you revoke access or request deletion, we will remove your data within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete that information promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
